Orbit Hop · Privacy

Ronda 32 Games · Last updated November 4, 2026 · Short version, a fuller policy will replace it.

What we keep

  • Your Telegram user ID, your first name (plus the initial of your last name) and your Telegram language, to show you on the daily leaderboard.
  • Your games, Daily and Practice: the moments you tapped, your score and how far you got, so the server can check the score and we can tune the difficulty.
  • Which days you opened the game, which link brought you in, how long the game was open and how often you paused.

We do not keep your phone number, your @username, your contacts, your IP address or your location. We do not sell data or show ads.

Why

To run the leaderboard, stop cheating, and see how many people play, how far they get and whether they come back, so we can make the game better. Reports use a code per player, never your name.

How long

  • Games: 30 days.
  • Everything else: 12 months.
  • If you do not open the game for 12 months, everything linked to you is deleted.

Delete your data

Tap Delete my data at the bottom of the game's menu: everything linked to your Telegram ID is erased at once. You can also message @ronda32games_bot and we will do it within 30 days.

Who runs the servers

The game is served from GitHub Pages and the server runs on Cloudflare. Telegram's own terms apply inside Telegram.
